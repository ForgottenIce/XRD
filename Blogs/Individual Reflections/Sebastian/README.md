## Augmented Reality Project Reflections
For the AR project, the goal was to create an application that helps users find their car in a parking lot in an engaging way. The concept involved overlaying a 3D model of the parking lot onto the real world, as seen through the phone's camera. The process began with performing 3D scans of the parking lot using an iPhone with LiDAR capabilities. These scans provided a foundation for creating a digital representation of the parking lot, though the accuracy was limitied, particualarly due to the large scale of the area. Precise measurments from external sources were later incorporated to enhance the model.

The 3D model was refined and scaled to match the real-world dimensions of the parking lot. Parking spots and lines were placed to ensure alignment with the actual layout. A calibration sign was modeled in accurate scale to act as a fixed reference point for synchronizing the virtual model with the physical environment. Additionally, arrow models were designed for navigation assistance within the app. Adjustments to the calibration sign's positioning were essential to improve tracking precision.

The tracking system for the 3D model relied on aligning it with a physical sign in the parking lot, which served as a reference point. This approach ensured the virtual model stayed accurately positioned in relation to the real-world environment. By using this calibration method, the app was able to effectively overlay the digital elements, resulting in an AR experience that felt intuitive and immersive. Users could easily interact with the application, and the tracking remained stable even when moving around the parking lot, demonstrating the reliability of this approach.

## Virtual Reality Project Reflections
In the VR project, the focus was on developing a Minecraft-inspired game where players navigate through a randomly generated maze while avoiding a pursuing Warden. The game incorporated several design and technical elements to create an engaging and immersive experience. The foundational components of the maze were created using custom-designed 3D building blocks. These elements were critical in achieving a consistent visual style and maintaining the Minecraft aesthetic. The maze's design was further enhanced with additional elements such as floors, ceilings, and a defined end area.

Models from Minecraft were adapted and optimized for Unity to ensure smooth integration into the game. Custom prefabs were developed to streamline the process of generating the maze layout. The game also featured a Minecraft-inspired lobby, designed to set the tone and immerse players from the start. This space served as a tutorial area where players could familiarize themselves with the controls and objectives before entering the maze.

Interactive 3D models were a core aspect of the gameplay. Elements such as buttons, levers, and snowballs were implemented with scripts to manage user interactions. These objects added depth to the gameplay by allowing players to directly engage with their environment. Sound effects, including footsteps and heartbeats, where integrated tn enhance the realism and tensiuon within the game.

To showcase the project, a video presentatiuon was produced, highlighting the game's features and gameplay mechanics. The presentation served as an effective way to communicate the project's vision.
